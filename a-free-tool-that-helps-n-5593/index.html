How to Check WAEC Results

Requirements to access your grades on the official portal when released:

  • 10-Digit WAEC Examination Number.
  • Scratch Card / e-PIN (Token) purchased via Interswitch or WAEC portal.
  • Exam Year and Type (School / Private GCE).

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the WAEC results be released?

The exact date varies each year. Our estimator provides the most common release window based on historical patterns.

Can I track multiple exam sessions?

Yes! Use the “Save to My Trackers” button to store as many sessions as you need. Access them under the “Trackers” tab.

Is there a fee to check my results?

You only need a valid WAEC token (scratch card or e‑PIN). No additional fees are required to view results on the official portal.
