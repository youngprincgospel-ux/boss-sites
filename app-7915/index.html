How to Share Offline (Like Xender)
1
Setup Hotspot

One phone turns on Wi-Fi Hotspot. The second phone connects to that Wi-Fi Hotspot. No mobile data or internet is used!

2
Open DropIt

Both phones open this same webpage in their mobile browsers (Chrome/Safari).

3
Connect & Send

Sender taps Send Files and generates the QR/Offer. Receiver taps Receive Files to complete the handshake. Instant lightning-fast direct file transfer!
