How to Check WAEC Results

Requirements to access your grades on the official portal when released:

  • 10-Digit WAEC Examination Number.
  • Scratch Card / e-PIN (Token) purchased via Interswitch or WAEC portal.
  • Exam Year and Type (School / Private GCE).

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these result dates 100% exact?+
They are precision-modeled estimates based on historical WAEC release patterns over the last 5 years in Nigeria.
Can I check my result directly on this app?+
No, this app provides accurate timelines, countdowns, and checklists. You check your actual grades on the official WAEC Nigeria portal.
How many times can a single e-PIN be used?+
A standard WAEC scratch card token can typically be used up to 5 times for a specific candidate number.
