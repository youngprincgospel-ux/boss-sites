About Velour & Co.

Founded in 2024, Velour & Co. bridges the gap between traditional Nigerian craftsmanship and contemporary global streetwear culture.

We source 100% organic cotton locally, empowering skilled tailors in Ikeja and Surulere while maintaining uncompromising international quality standards. Every stitch tells a story of ambition, resilience, and style.
